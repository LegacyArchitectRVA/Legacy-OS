# LegacyOS Operational Intelligence

## Purpose

Transform stored business knowledge into actionable operational guidance.

## Decision Flow

1. Receive business question
2. Collect workspace context
3. Evaluate risks
4. Generate recommendation
5. Assign confidence score
6. Track resulting action

## Core Capabilities

- Decision support
- Risk awareness
- Action tracking
- Continuity recommendations

Future additions:

- Historical decision comparison
- Automated alerts
- Process improvement suggestions
- Continuity risk scoring
