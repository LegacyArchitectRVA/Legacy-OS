# LegacyOS Brand System

## Brand Position

LegacyOS is the operational continuity system behind the philosophy of "Order in Your Absence."

The brand should feel like:
- A trusted advisor
- A private executive office
- A heritage institution
- A premium continuity solution

Avoid:
- Generic AI aesthetics
- Neon technology themes
- Robot imagery
- Startup gimmicks

## Typography

Primary Display:
- Bodoni Moda inspired serif
- Used for titles, major statements, and brand moments

Secondary:
- Garamond inspired serif
- Used for editorial content and long-form communication

Interface:
- Clean modern sans-serif
- Used for dashboards, controls, and data

## Visual Language

Themes:
- Shield
- Compass
- Archive
- Vault
- Blueprint
- Continuity
- Order

Icons should be:
- Simple
- Premium
- Recognizable at small sizes
- Consistent line weight

## Product Icons

Business Brain:
Knowledge archive + compass

Legacy Score:

## Logo Direction

LegacyOS master mark should connect visually with Legacy Architect RVA while remaining a standalone product identity.

Core symbolism:
A compass within a shield representing guidance, preparedness, and continuity.
