# LegacyOS Continuity Engine

## Purpose

The Continuity Engine measures whether a business or household can continue operating when a key person becomes unavailable.

## Initial Assessment Areas

- Digital access
- Financial operations
- Property and assets
- Health and personal information
- Legal documentation
- Business processes
- Legacy instructions

## Output

The engine produces:

- Readiness score
- Missing information
- Risk areas
- Recommended next actions

## Future Expansion

- Automated readiness questionnaires
- AI-generated continuity plans
- Scenario simulations
- Successor guidance
