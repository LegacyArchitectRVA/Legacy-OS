# LegacyOS Database Foundation

## Purpose

The LegacyOS database preserves business knowledge, operating procedures, and continuity information.

## Core Tables

- workspaces: business operating environments
- workspace_members: access and permissions
- business_profiles: identity, mission, and operating context
- knowledge_documents: uploaded business knowledge
- sops: repeatable processes and procedures
- ai_memories: learned operational context
- activity_logs: system history and audit trail
