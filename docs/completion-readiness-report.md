# LegacyOS Completion Readiness Report

## Product Systems

- Business Brain: defined as the knowledge and SOP management layer.
- Executive Advisor: defined as the decision review layer.
- Production Assistant: defined as the consistency and workflow layer.
- Continuity Vault: defined as the operational continuity layer.
- Legacy Score: defined as the readiness measurement layer.

## Final Implementation Requirements

Before production launch:

- Connect authentication provider.
- Connect persistent database.
- Connect secure document storage.
- Add AI retrieval with source grounding.
- Add automated tests.
- Validate permissions and tenant isolation.
- Complete deployment verification.

## Release Standard

LegacyOS should only provide operational guidance from approved business knowledge and should preserve source traceability for important decisions.
