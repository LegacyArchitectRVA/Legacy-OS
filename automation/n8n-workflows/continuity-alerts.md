# Continuity Alerts Workflow

## Purpose
Identify operational risks before they become failures.

## Trigger
- Scheduled weekly review
- New critical document change
- Missing required business information

## Actions
1. Evaluate readiness score
2. Identify missing continuity items
3. Notify authorized users
4. Create recommended tasks
5. Log activity history

## Integrations
- n8n
- Supabase
- Email provider
- CRM systems
