# LegacyOS

## Order in Your Absence

LegacyOS is an AI-powered business continuity operating system designed to preserve knowledge, systems, SOPs, workflows, and decision-making.

The mission is simple: a business should continue operating even when leadership changes or becomes unavailable.

## Core Modules

- Business Brain: centralized knowledge, SOPs, templates, and operational memory
- Executive Advisor: decision analysis, risk evaluation, and assumption testing
- Production Assistant: content workflows, brand consistency, and publishing systems
- Continuity Vault: critical business information and operational readiness
- Legacy Score: measuring business continuity preparedness

## Product Philosophy

LegacyOS applies the same philosophy as Legacy Architect RVA's Life Manual:

People and businesses should have a clear path forward when someone important is unavailable.
